Privacy Policy

Idle Pioneer · by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} · Effective {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

The short version. Idle Pioneer has no accounts, no chat and no analytics. We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. Your game progress stays on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and purchases go through Google Play or the Apple App Store; those companies may collect some data, as explained below and in their own policies.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the mobile game Idle Pioneer for Android and iOS (the "Game"), published by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} ("we", "us"). You can reach us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

2. What the Game itself collects

Nothing that leaves your device. The Game is single-player. It has no sign-in, no player profiles, no chat, no user-generated content and no analytics or crash-reporting service of our own. We do not receive your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information.

The Game saves your progress (colonies, resources, settings and similar game state) in a file in the app's private storage on your device. This file is never sent to us.

Save codes. In Settings you can choose Copy save code, which copies a text version of your progress to your device's clipboard, and Restore from code, which reads one back. This happens only when you tap those buttons. Where you paste or keep that code is up to you; we never see it.

3. Data collected by third parties

Two kinds of third-party services are built into the Game. They run under their own privacy policies, and we do not control what they do with data.

Advertising: Google AdMob

The free version of the Game shows ads through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google states that the SDK may collect:

DataWhat it is
Device identifiersThe Android advertising ID or app set ID, or on iOS the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking) and the identifier for vendors.
IP addressUsed to estimate your general (city- or region-level) location. Not precise GPS location.
Ad interactionsFor example app launches, ads shown, taps and video views.
DiagnosticsPerformance and crash information about the app and the ad SDK, such as launch time or hang rate.

Google says this data is encrypted in transit. Depending on your region and your choices, ads may be personalized (based on your ad identifier and activity in other apps and sites) or non-personalized (based on context and general location only).

The optional "Ad-Free" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple's App Store (iOS). They handle your payment details and account. We never receive your card number or other payment information. The store only tells the Game whether you own the Ad-Free item so it can unlock it.

4. Why this data is used

  • Your save file: to keep your progress between sessions.
  • Ad data (by Google): to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, and detect fraud and abuse. Ads help keep the Game free.
  • Purchase data (by Google or Apple): to complete and verify your purchase and let you restore it.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use any data for our own profiling.

5. Your choices

Consent and privacy options

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the Game shows Google's consent message before personalized ads are used. You can review or change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy choices in the Game.

Tracking on iOS

On iPhone and iPad, the Game may ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). This only happens if tracking is used at all. Saying no never limits the Game: you still get the full game, with non-personalized ads. You can change this later in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Your advertising ID

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the device's Settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads). On iOS, turning off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" stops apps from accessing the advertising identifier.

Remove ads

The one-time Ad-Free purchase removes the ads that play automatically. The optional rewarded bonus can then be claimed without watching an ad.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your save file stays on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the Game deletes it, along with its backup copy. (Some devices may include app data in your own device backups, such as Google or iCloud backups, which you control.) A save code you copied exists only wherever you put it.

Data collected by Google or Apple is kept according to their own policies; see the links above.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Our ad requests are not marked as child-directed. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help, including by passing the request to Google or Apple.

8. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising.

  • Because we hold no personal information about you, we usually have nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. Your save data is only on your device, and you can delete it by uninstalling the Game.
  • For ad data, you can withdraw or change consent in Settings > Privacy choices, reset your advertising ID, or deny tracking on iOS. You can also make requests to Google directly through your Google Account or the Google Privacy Policy.
  • For purchase data, contact Google or Apple through their privacy pages.

You can also email us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We will answer, explain what (if anything) we hold, and point you to the right company. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights. If you are in the EEA or UK, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the Game operates under EU or UK law, Google's processing of ad data for personalized advertising relies on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above.
