Saving and backups

How does saving work?

The game saves automatically, on your device only. There's no account and no cloud save. The game also keeps a backup of your last good save, so a crash or a full disk shouldn't cost you progress.

Because the save lives on your device, uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes your progress. Back it up with a save code first.

How do I back up my progress?

Open Settings and tap Copy save code. A text code is copied to your clipboard. Paste it somewhere safe, such as a note or an email to yourself. The code holds your whole game, so keep it private if you don't want others to have a copy of your colonies.

How do I restore progress or move to a new phone?
  1. On the old device: Settings > Copy save code, and keep the code somewhere you can reach from the new device.
  2. On the new device: install Idle Pioneer, copy the code to the clipboard, then open Settings > Restore from code.

Restoring replaces the progress on that device, so the game asks you to confirm first. Time between copying and restoring doesn't count as offline time.

Can I play on Android and iPhone with the same save?

Yes. Save codes work on both. Purchases don't carry across stores, though: see Restore Purchases below.

Offline progress

Do my colonies keep working while I'm away?

Yes. When you come back, your colonies will have kept producing and shipping at half their normal pace, for up to 4 hours of absence. Some upgrades raise that limit. Time beyond the limit isn't counted, so check in now and then.

I changed my device clock and got no offline progress.

Offline time only counts beyond the latest time the game has already seen. Moving the clock back and forth won't grant extra progress. Once real time passes that point again, offline progress works as normal.

Ads, Ad-Free and purchases

When do ads appear?

The game is free. Ads never play when you open the game. An ad may appear between expeditions, and there's one optional rewarded ad you can watch for a bonus. You never have to watch it.

What does Ad-Free do?

Ad-Free is a one-time purchase (US$4.99, or the local equivalent shown by your store). It removes the ads that play on their own, and lets you claim the optional rewarded bonus without watching an ad. It isn't a subscription.

I bought Ad-Free but still see ads, or I reinstalled or got a new phone.

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Google Play account or Apple ID that made the purchase. Purchases belong to your store account, not your save, so Ad-Free made on Google Play doesn't carry over to iPhone (or the other way round).

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. On Android, see Google Play's refund help. On iOS, use reportaproblem.apple.com. We never see your payment details.

Privacy

What data does the game collect?

We don't collect any personal data. The ad and purchase services from Google and Apple may, as explained in our Privacy Policy.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings > Privacy choices to review or change your ad consent (where your region requires it). On iPhone, you can also allow or deny tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the device's Settings. Refusing tracking or personalized ads never limits the game.
